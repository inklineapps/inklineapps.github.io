Sharp Mermaid

Mermaid diagrams that stay sharp. SVG, zoomable, always current. For Confluence Cloud.

Paste your Mermaid source, get a proper diagram — full width, zoomable, and readable. Diagrams live as text inside your page, so they are searchable, diffable, and easy to keep up to date.

What it does

  • Sharp, not blurry. Diagrams render as true SVG, so they stay crisp at any zoom. Zoom in, drag to pan, or expand to full width for large architecture diagrams.
  • Always current. Ships with Mermaid 11 and is kept up to date, so newer diagram types work the day they land.
  • No account. No limits. Everyone on your site can create diagrams. No separate sign-up, no cap on how many diagrams you make.
  • Follows your theme. Light and dark Confluence both look right, with no setting to change.
  • Nothing leaves Atlassian. Rendering happens in your browser. We never receive a copy of your diagrams.

Supported diagram types

Everything Mermaid 11 supports: flowcharts, sequence, class, state, entity-relationship, user journey, Gantt, pie, quadrant, requirement, gitgraph, mindmap, timeline, sankey, XY chart, block, packet, architecture and kanban.

Requirements

Confluence Cloud. Data Center is not supported.
