Sharp Mermaid — Diagrams for Confluence

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Summary

Sharp Mermaid renders diagrams inside your Confluence pages. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, and it makes no network requests to anything outside Atlassian.

What the app stores

The only thing Sharp Mermaid stores is the Mermaid source text you type into a diagram. That text is saved by Confluence as part of the macro's configuration, inside your own Confluence page, on Atlassian's infrastructure. Inkline Apps never receives a copy.

What the app does not do

  • Does not collect personal information, names, email addresses or account identifiers
  • Does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising
  • Does not send data to Inkline Apps or any third party
  • Does not call any external service — diagrams are rendered entirely in your browser using a bundled copy of the Mermaid library
  • Does not require an account, sign-up or login of any kind

Where processing happens

Rendering happens locally in your browser. The app runs on Atlassian Forge, so any data it touches stays within Atlassian's infrastructure.

Data retention and deletion

Diagram source lives in your Confluence page. Delete the macro or the page and it is gone. Uninstalling the app removes it from your site. Inkline Apps holds nothing to delete.

Permissions

The app requests no Confluence API scopes. It reads only the configuration of the macro it is rendering.
