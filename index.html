Sharp Mermaid — Diagrams for Confluence

Render Mermaid diagrams as crisp, zoomable SVG inside your Confluence pages. Zoom in, drag to pan, expand for large architecture diagrams. No account, no diagram limits, and nothing leaves Atlassian.

  • Sharp, not blurry — true SVG, readable at any zoom
  • Mermaid 11 — newer diagram types work the day they land
  • Follows your theme — light and dark Confluence both look right
  • Nothing leaves Atlassian — rendering happens in your browser
